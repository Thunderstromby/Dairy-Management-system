## Brand & Style

This design system blends cinematic precision farming with the poised elegance of a heritage botanical publication. Designed for high-performing agricultural operators, agronomists, and estate managers, the aesthetic discards raw utilitarian dashboard tropes in favor of an intentional, editorial calm. 

The emotional signature is grounded, tactile, and quiet:
- **Cinematic Stewardship:** Panoramic visual ratios, measured compositions, and a contemplative pace that mirrors high-level landscape management.
- **Editorial Rigor:** Typographic authority through balanced serif accents paired with surgical sans-serif data hierarchy.
- **Organic Precision:** Earth-derived pigmentations, gentle pill shapes, and subtle framing lines evoking agricultural contours, crop rows, and architectural glasshouses.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in agricultural terroir, rejecting synthetic neon digital greens in favor of authentic vegetative depths and fertile tones:

- **Warm Ivory (`#F7F7F1`):** The primary canvas foundation. It avoids stark sterile monitor whites, producing an absorbent, paper-like baseline.
- **Deep Forest (`#183F30`):** The dominant primary hue. Used for core interactive elements, high-emphasis icons, and structural anchor blocks.
- **Pasture Green (`#397A58`):** The secondary chromatic step. Applied to active states, secondary actions, and visual progression meters.
- **Sage Mist (`#E9EFE5`):** The structural containment layer. Used for subtle card fills, interactive hover backdrops, and muted dividers.
- **Deep Charcoal (`#25332C`):** The high-contrast neutral for primary typography and dense data visualization, softening absolute black into a botanical shade.
- **Harvest Gold (`#D5A64A`):** The focused accent. Reserved strictly for yield notifications, threshold cautions, sensor metrics, and verified harvest badges.
- **White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity floating card surfaces and nested modal viewports.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes architectural technical clarity with bespoke editorial flourish:

- **Instrument Serif:** The narrative anchor. Deployed selectively for editorial chapter headers, field section titles, seasonal climate overviews, and high-impact introductory statements. It must never be used for functional data labels or buttons.
- **Manrope:** The structural voice. Provides high-clarity headlines, field zone identifiers, and metric indicators with robust legibility across varying device screens.
- **DM Sans:** The operational workhorse. Low-contrast geometric balance ensures long-form yield logs, sensor readouts, and navigation controls remain frictionless and ergonomic.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors expansive farmland: deliberate, spacious, and uncluttered.

- **Grid Strategy:** A fluid 12-column system on desktop and tablet, collapsing into a 4-column system on mobile devices.
- **Whitespace Cadence:** Canvas areas favor breathing room (`space-xl` and `margin`) to prevent sensory fatigue when parsing complex agricultural telemetries (soil moisture, canopy temperature, weather trajectories).
- **Asymmetric Tension:** Key field metrics and panoramic crop maps utilize wide editorial hero containers, juxtaposed against tighter, compact data blocks arranged along clean gridlines.
- **Responsive Adaptations:** 
  - Desktop (>1024px): 48px canvas margins, 24px gutters, generous vertical section spacing.
  - Tablet (768px - 1023px): 32px canvas margins, 20px gutters.
  - Mobile (<768px): 20px margins, 16px gutters, sequential single-column card flows with vertical rhythm dictated by `space-md` and `space-lg`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects artificial dropshadows and heavy blurs, achieving depth through tonal layering, crisp borders, and ambient diffusion:

- **Tonal Stepping:** Depth is achieved by placing Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Sage Mist (`#E9EFE5`) planes against the Warm Ivory (`#F7F7F1`) canvas.
- **Hairline Framing:** Cards and floating panels utilize ultra-subtle, organic hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(24, 63, 48, 0.08)` or `1px solid #E9EFE5`) rather than opaque outlines, evoking classic botanical drafting grids.
- **Ambient Terroir Shadows:** Floating drawers and modal dialogues employ ultra-diffused, ground-tinted shadow envelopes: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(37, 51, 44, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(37, 51, 44, 0.03)`. No pure black or synthetic cyan shadow values.

## Shapes

The shape system centers on pill geometry (`roundedness: 3`) contrasted against gently curved container canvases:

- **Pills (`9999px`):** Used universally for all interactive triggers, buttons, search inputs, tabs, and status badges. This creates an organic, river-pebble tactility that feels comfortable under touch in field environments.
- **Structural Radii:** Content panels, field cards, and modal canvases utilize refined corners (`rounded-lg: 2rem` or `rounded-xl: 3rem`), mirroring softened terraced landscapes rather than rigid electronic quadrants.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Pill-shaped, Deep Forest background (`#183F30`), crisp Warm Ivory text (`#F7F7F1`), `0 2px 4px rgba(24, 63, 48, 0.15)`. Hover transitions to Pasture Green (`#397A58`).
- **Secondary:** Pill-shaped, transparent background with a 1px border of Deep Forest (`#183F30`) at 20% opacity. Text is Deep Forest. Hover shifts surface to Sage Mist (`#E9EFE5`).
- **Harvest Action (Accent):** Pill-shaped, Harvest Gold fill (`#D5A64A`) with Deep Charcoal text (`#25332C`), reserved for crucial seasonal milestones (e.g., "Initiate Harvest", "Deploy Irrigation").

### Chips & Status Pills
- **Status Indicators:** Pill-shaped enclosures with miniature interior dot indicators. 
  - Optimal / Active: Sage Mist background (`#E9EFE5`) with Pasture Green dot and Deep Charcoal text.
  - Alert / Harvest: Soft Harvest Gold tint (`rgba(213, 166, 74, 0.15)`) with Harvest Gold dot and Deep Charcoal text.
- **Filter Chips:** Pill shape with 1px border (`#E9EFE5`). Selected state adopts Deep Forest fill with Warm Ivory text.

### Cards & Data Containers
- **Base Card:** Clean Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) surface, 24px (`rounded-lg`) border radius, bounded by a 1px hairline border of Sage Mist (`#E9EFE5`).
- **Editorial Spotlight Card:** Sage Mist (`#E9EFE5`) canvas with large Instrument Serif narrative titles, used for weather warnings, seasonal summaries, and agronomic insights.

### Form Inputs
- **Text & Search Fields:** Full pill enclosures (`9999px`), Pure White background, inset padding (`14px 20px`), subtle border (`1px solid rgba(24, 63, 48, 0.12)`). Focused state deepens border to Deep Forest (`#183F30`) with no harsh outer glow.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Radio Buttons:** Smooth circular pills with 1.5px Deep Charcoal outlines; inner dot fills with Deep Forest when selected.
- **Checkboxes:** Slightly rounded squircle (`4px` radius) to match botanical geometry, filling with Deep Forest and displaying a hairline ivory check mark.

### Specialized Agricultural Components
- **Field Metric Dial:** Minimal circular rings rendering soil moisture and NDVI indexes with Pasture Green progression tracks over Warm Ivory baselines.
- **Crop Stage Timeline:** Linear horizontal bar punctuated by pill badges indicating germination, vegetative growth, flowering, and maturation stages.
