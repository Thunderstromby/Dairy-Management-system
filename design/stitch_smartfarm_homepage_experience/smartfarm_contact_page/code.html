Security & Architecture

How SmartFarm access is arranged.

To ensure data integrity, biometric isolation, and uninterrupted sensor telemetry across milking parlors, SmartFarm maintains a strict, managed onboarding protocol.

contact_mail

Prospective customers contact the SmartFarm team.

All engagements start with an operational conversation to establish pasture boundaries and hardware interfaces.

admin_panel_settings

Customer registration and workspace creation are handled by the Super Admin.

Our central deployment architects securely provision the dedicated instance, telemetry endpoints, and paddock schemas.

badge

A Farm Admin account is provisioned for the customer.

The primary farm owner or operations director receives master credentials to manage staff roles, RFID tags, and vat alerts.

block

There is no public self-registration or automatic online purchase.

Protected agrarian infrastructure requires authenticated configuration to guarantee reliable field performance.
