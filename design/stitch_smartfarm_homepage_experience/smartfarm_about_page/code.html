The Operating Reality

One farm. Many moving parts.
One connected view.

Modern dairy farms handle hundreds of operational events each day. Animal identifications, vaccination regimes, bulk milk yield meters, feed inventory stores, milking maintenance logs, and worker rosters are constantly evolving.

Yet on many holdings, this crucial data remains scattered across physical whiteboards, separate spreadsheets, and siloed machinery screens. SmartFarm was built to eliminate the gap between what is happening in the sheds and pastures and what farm operators can immediately see and verify.

Built from muddy boots and real yard hours, not theoretical software abstractions.

Practical by design

Agricultural Roles

Designed around the people managing the farm.

SmartFarm acknowledges the real hierarchy of dairy agriculture. Not every member of the team needs the same view, but everyone benefits from centralized accuracy.

ADM

Farm Owners & Administrators

Manage farm structures, oversee multi-facility operations, review financial telemetry, configure custom veterinary rules, and access the executive tools available within their dedicated workspace.

SUP

Farm Supervisors & Herd Managers

Work directly with day-to-day operational tasks, record milking weights, execute breeding schedules, and log pasture conditions according to their assigned supervisor permissions.

Role-based access engineered for farm hierarchy — operational staff are recorded and managed seamlessly without unnecessary system logins or redundant software seats.
