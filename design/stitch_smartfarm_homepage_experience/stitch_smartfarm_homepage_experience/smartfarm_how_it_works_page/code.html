STAGE 01 // ONBOARDING & SETUP

Start with your farm workspace.

Every dairy enterprise has unique grazing boundaries, herd numbers, milking shifts, and tank capacities. Rather than leaving you to generic self-service menus, our deployment specialists personally establish your production instance.

  • 1

    Direct Specialist Consultation

    A prospective customer contacts the SmartFarm team to map out physical infrastructure and dairy requirements.

  • 2

    Tenant Isolation & Baseline Provisioning

    The SmartFarm technical team handles enterprise registration, data isolation, and farm geometry configuration.

  • 3

    Farm Admin Issuance

    A primary Farm Admin account is provisioned by the Super Admin with verified credentials and cryptographic tokens.

  • 4

    Workspace Handover

    The designated Farm Admin receives immediate direct access to their isolated, fully configured dairy instance.

info

Operational Policy: There is NO public self-registration, automated card checkout, unverified trial provisioning, or automated tenant generation. Every instance undergoes strict tenant verification.
