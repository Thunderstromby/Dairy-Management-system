## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a high-performance, grounded visual language for modern precision agriculture. It merges the tactile dignity of cinematic pastoral landscapes with the clarity, efficiency, and intelligence of mission-critical field operations software.

### Tone & Personality
- **Natural & Dependable:** Rooted in biological rhythms, earth sciences, and pastoral stewardship. Visuals avoid artificial tech sterility in favor of organic stability and structural calm.
- **Intelligent & Confident:** High signal-to-noise ratio. Telemetry data, soil metrics, and livestock vital signs are delivered with authoritative editorial weight.
- **Modern & Premium Agritech:** Completely rejects generic SaaS electric blues and superficial glassmorphism. Surfaces reflect tactile materiality, functional restraint, and durable field instrumentation.

### Visual Style
A tailored hybrid of **Modern Editorial Agritech** and **Tactile Utility**:
- **Field-Ready Density:** Balanced white space that allows expansive data scanning without feeling bloated.
- **Durable Containers:** Subtle structural strokes, warm earthen canvases, and sharp typographic heirarchies replacing decorative blurs.
- **Restrained Vitality:** Harvest Gold acts strictly as an operational spark (warnings, vital metrics, key milestones), never as background noise.

## Colors
The palette evokes rich topsoil, flourishing pastures, and grain ready for harvest, anchored by natural light surfaces.

### Primary Roles
- **Primary (`#183F30` - Deep Forest):** Anchor tone for high-emphasis actions, top-level navigation headers, primary buttons, and heavy structural anchors.
- **Secondary (`#397A58` - Pasture Green):** Supporting operational states, active toggles, positive agricultural trends, and secondary badges.
- **Tertiary (`#D5A64A` - Harvest Gold):** An intentional highlight reserved for critical telemetry, alerts, active sensor indicators, and yield milestones. Never overused.
- **Neutral Primary (`#25332C` - Deep Charcoal):** Grounded text color ensuring superior optical contrast without the harshness of pure black.

### Surface & Atmosphere
- **Canvas Base (`#F7F7F1` - Warm Ivory):** The default root background. Imparts an organic, warm paper feel that mitigates screen fatigue during long outdoor operations.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Used for elevated interactive cards, data tables, and input panels to establish clear visual planes.
- **Surface Accent (`#E9EFE5` - Sage Mist):** Subtle tint used for chip backgrounds, selected list rows, and telemetry metric backgrounds.
- **Border Default (`#DCE3DA`):** Low-contrast structural dividing line for cards, dividers, and data table columns.

### Text Tiers
- **Primary Text:** `#25332C` (Deep Charcoal) - Body text, high-level headers, and active values.
- **Secondary Text:** `#58675D` - Metadata, field labels, and secondary context.
- **Muted Text:** `#849087` - Inactive states, timestamps, placeholder text, and structural units.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm pairs the architectural balance of **Manrope** for authoritative statements and summaries with the neutral precision of **Inter** for intensive operational data and operational workflows.

### Application Rules
- **Manrope:** Restricted to high-level system indicators, card headlines, and metric values. Metric numbers (`tabular-nums`) use Manrope with semi-bold to bold weights to create instant ocular hierarchy across dense telemetry dashboards.
- **Inter:** Serves all functional controls, analytical tables, body copy, and status flags. Inter maintains extreme legibility at small sizes (`11px` - `12px`), crucial when displaying hardware IDs, pasture moisture levels, or dosage parameters.
- **Numbers & Data:** Tabular figures must be enabled across all data-dense grids and sensor feeds to prevent layout shifts during live polling.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column responsive grid engineered for operational workstations, rugged field tablets, and pocket devices.

### Grid & Layout Strategy
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid, `2rem` margin, `1.5rem` gutter. Permits dual-pane spatial layouts (e.g., live paddock satellite maps alongside herd telemetry cards).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column fluid grid, `1.5rem` margin, `1rem` gutter. Secondary navigation collapses into a consolidated top rail.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` edge margins and `0.75rem` internal gutters. Primary action controls anchor directly to thumb-reachable zones at the screen bottom.

### Component Spacing Rhythm
- **Micro Spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`):** Reserved for chip padding, badge icons, input field internal vertical alignment, and nested status values.
- **Standard Spacing (`space-md`, `space-lg`):** Standard internal padding for cards, module stacks, and form row intervals.
- **Structural Separation (`space-xl`):** Section-to-section breaks and broad canvas separations.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation rejects fake blur techniques and heavy synthetic drop shadows. Instead, it relies on **tonal stepping** and **quiet ambient occlusion** that replicates physical hardware displays operating under natural light.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F7F7F1` (Warm Ivory). Completely flat, grounding all views.
2. **Level 1 (Structural Containers & Cards):** `#FFFFFF` (White) resting on `#F7F7F1`. Delineated primarily by a crisp `1px` stroke in `#DCE3DA`. Shadow is subtle and warm: `0 1px 3px rgba(37, 51, 44, 0.04)`.
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Floating & Context Panels):** `#FFFFFF` bounded by `#DCE3DA`, elevated via `0 4px 12px rgba(37, 51, 44, 0.08)`. Used for dropdown menus, active popovers, and quick-filter modals.
4. **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Critical Alerts):** Raised above an earthen scrim (`#183F30` at 40% opacity). Elevation cast: `0 12px 32px rgba(24, 63, 48, 0.12)`.

## Shapes
The design adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** corner philosophy. The subtle radii (`0.25rem` standard, `0.5rem` for cards/panels, `0.75rem` for large modals) signal engineering precision, tactile hardware durability, and dependable software architecture. Completely circular pills and aggressive rounded geometries are rejected to avoid a juvenile consumer appearance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#183F30` background, pure white text, `0.25rem` radius, `0.5rem` vertical by `1rem` horizontal padding. Hover shifts to `#397A58`. Active state scales down slightly (`0.99`). Focus ring uses a `2px` offset stroke in `#397A58`.
- **Secondary:** `#FFFFFF` background with `1px` solid `#DCE3DA` border, text in `#25332C`. Hover initiates a background transition to `#E9EFE5`.
- **Tertiary / Subdued:** Ghost button with `#25332C` text and no default background; hover applies `#E9EFE5` fill.
- **Accent / Alert (Harvest Gold):** `#D5A64A` background with `#25332C` text. Exclusively for direct alerts or urgent farm interventions.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Compact labels with `0.25rem` radius, `0.25rem` vertical and `0.5rem` horizontal padding. Typography set in `label-sm` uppercase.
  - *Optimal / Active:* `#E9EFE5` surface, `#397A58` text.
  - *Caution / Inspection Needed:* `#FBF4E4` surface, `#9A7222` text.
  - *Offline / Archived:* `#F7F7F1` surface, `#849087` text, `#DCE3DA` border.
- **Filter Chips:** Interactive toggles using `#FFFFFF` background and `#DCE3DA` border; when active, transitions to `#183F30` background with pure white text.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 40px, `#FFFFFF` background, `1px` solid `#DCE3DA` border, `0.25rem` radius. Placeholder text in `#849087`. Focus state shifts border directly to `#397A58` with a 2px soft halo.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `16px` squared (`0.125rem` radius) or circular elements with a `1.5px` border in `#58675D`. Checked state fills with `#183F30` and displays a crisp white checkmark.
- **Toggles / Switches:** Width 36px, height 20px. Off state uses `#DCE3DA`; On state illuminates in `#397A58`. Handle is pure white with zero blur.

### Cards & Data Panels
- **Standard Card:** Pure white background, `0.5rem` radius, framed with a continuous `1px` border of `#DCE3DA`. Internal card header separated by a matching divider line.
- **Metric Highlight Card:** Card with a top accent edge (3px solid `#397A58` or `#D5A64A`). Employs a large `Manrope` stat display (`headline-lg`) paired with an `Inter` micro-label in `#58675D`.

### Domain-Specific Agricultural Components
- **Livestock / Paddock Status Table:** Alternating rows using `#FFFFFF` and `#F7F7F1` with hover feedback in `#E9EFE5`. Numerical sensor readouts (temperature, somatic cell count, soil moisture) aligned using tabular numerals.
- **Telemetry Gauge:** Clean radial or linear bar track in `#E9EFE5`, with fill progress in `#397A58` and peak warning threshold markers rendered in `#D5A64A`.
