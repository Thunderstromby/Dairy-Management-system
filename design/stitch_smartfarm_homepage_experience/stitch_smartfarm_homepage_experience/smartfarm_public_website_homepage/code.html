Simple Implementation

A clearer way to manage your farm.

A dependable onboarding structure adapted directly to your dairy facilities.

01

Set up your farm

The farm workspace is prepared and provisioned by the SmartFarm team. Your herd structures, milking parlor setup, and paddock boundaries are configured for your specific location.

02

Organize your operations

Farm information and daily operational records are managed through the platform. Milking collections, rations, health checks, and worker shifts are recorded in real time.

03

Manage with greater clarity

Connected records and reporting help farm management understand operations. Identify yield variances, audit environmental compliance, and make confident seasonal decisions.
