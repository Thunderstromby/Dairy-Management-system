## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-tier executive administration environment tailored for agricultural technology infrastructure, autonomous field fleets, and global yield telemetry. The aesthetic moves away from conventional vivid green "eco" tropes, leaning instead into an earthy, restrained, and utilitarian luxury that echoes precision agronomy, rich soil chemistry, and field-grade hardware engineering.

### Design Tone
- **Dignified & Pragmatic:** Prioritizes high information density and cognitive endurance over decorative flourish.
- **Architectural & Terrestrial:** Uses natural stone tones, deep botanical olive, and warm copper accents to bridge soil-level physics with computational rigor.
- **Precision Data Ecology:** Emphasizes crisp alignment, tabular fidelity, and disciplined typographic hierarchy to support mission-critical decisions across distributed farmland.

### Aesthetic Movement
Modern Executive Brutalism tempered by Organic Tactility. Surfaces are flat, structured, and demarcated by low-contrast, hairline stone borders rather than heavy ambient shadows. Visual hierarchy is established through surface tonal shifts, calibrated typography, and deliberate semantic color triggers.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in muted earth pigments and warm mineral tones, providing comfortable contrast for prolonged data analysis.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary Olive (`#46583B`):** Serves as the primary anchor for dominant structural elements, active tab indicators, and high-priority primary actions. Never used in massive, overwhelming slabs.
- **Secondary Muted Sage (`#89977A`):** Used for supporting categorical badges, inactive selection rings, metric graph baselines, and secondary controls.
- **Tertiary Warm Copper (`#B8794B`):** Deliberate focal accent reserved for critical callouts, key telemetric thresholds, and micro-interactions requiring immediate human intervention without signaling an alert state.
- **Neutral Charcoal (`#292D28`):** The primary text color, delivering reading contrast against stone surfaces without the harsh glare of true black.

### Surfaces & Boundaries
- **Main Canvas (`#F4F2EC`):** Warm stone background across full application viewports.
- **Card & Elevated Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-density workspace containers, tables, charts, and modal frames.
- **Recessed / Secondary Surface (`#EAE8E0`):** Table headers, panel drawers, filter bars, and nested metadata cards.
- **Hairline Border (`#DFDED5`):** Structural partition color across all cards, table rows, and input perimeters.

### Secondary Typography & Semantics
- **Text Secondary (`#697067`):** Field labels, subtitle metadata, and breadcrumbs.
- **Text Muted (`#92968D`):** Table pagination details, disabled states, and auxiliary units of measure.
- **Success (`#527A59`):** Forest-tinted operational confirmations, optimal soil moisture readings, and regular telemetry pings.
- **Warning (`#C18A42`):** Weather warnings, irrigation threshold anomalies, and maintenance notices.
- **Error (`#B9534D`):** Hardware disconnection, valve failure, and sensor degradation alerts.

## Typography

The type system runs on Plus Jakarta Sans, selected for its clean legibility, balanced geometry, and refined tabular rendering.

### Application Rules
- **Tabular Alignment:** When rendering sensor telemetry, GPS coordinates, flow rates, and numeric columns, apply `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` alongside the `tabular-data` token.
- **Letter Spacing:** Maintain negative tracking on display and major headlines to keep titles compact. Maintain subtle positive tracking on uppercase labels, state badges, and table column headers.
- **Hierarchy Restraint:** Limit dashboards to a maximum of three simultaneous type levels per modular viewport to avoid visual clutter during field operations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is a structured, fixed-inset dashboard grid designed to maintain high spatial efficiency across multi-monitor control hubs and tablet devices.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column grid with a fixed `margin-desktop` of `2rem` and `gutter-desktop` of `1.5rem`. Max container bound of 1600px centered on screen.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with `gutter` of `1.25rem` and `margin` of `1.5rem`. Sidebar collapses to an icon-only dock.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked flow with a rigid `1rem` outer canvas boundary. Sensor lists reflow to compact accordion structures.

### Structural Flow
- Internal component groupings use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for tight field-to-label bindings.
- Metric clusters within summary tiles utilize `space-md`.
- Component sections, analytical charting blocks, and data grids rely on `space-lg` and `space-xl` separation intervals.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual separation relies on tonal layering and hairline delineation rather than diffuse shadows. This prevents optical haziness and screen mud during sunny outdoor field deployments.

### Depth Mechanics
- **Ground Floor:** The canvas base sits at `#F4F2EC`.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Modules, Sidebars):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a solid 1px border (`#DFDED5`). Shadows are completely eliminated, except for a subtle ambient drop on high-density cards (`0 1px 2px rgba(41, 45, 40, 0.04)`).
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Popovers, Flyout Panels):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a 1px border (`#DFDED5`) accompanied by a crisp, restrained drop shadow (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(41, 45, 40, 0.08)`).
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Critical Interrupts):** Centered `#FFFFFF` containers bounded by `#DFDED5`, floating over an active scrim overlay of `#292D28` at 40% opacity.
- **Sunken State:** Filter inputs, segmented control tracks, and table headers adopt `#EAE8E0` with an inset or flat border treatment.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, architectural geometry (Level 1). Radii are controlled and understated to reinforce an industrial, equipment-grade visual language.

### Geometry Guidelines
- **Base Components:** Inputs, buttons, segmented toggles, and status chips apply a default corner radius of `0.25rem` (4px).
- **Cards & Data Tables:** Elevated module containers implement `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- **Floating Modals & Flyouts:** Enclosed containers scale up to `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px).
- **Status Indicators:** Micro pings and active connection lights retain full circular execution (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **No Pill Buttons:** Avoid oversized circular pills for high-level administrative buttons; geometry must remain squared and architectural.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#46583B`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none. Hover state transitions to `#3A4A31`. Active state reaches `#303E29`.
- **Secondary:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#292D28`, border 1px solid `#DFDED5`. Hover transitions to `#F4F2EC`.
- **Tertiary / Accent:** Background `#B8794B`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none. Reserved for trigger actions (e.g., "Deploy Irrigation Cycle", "Calibrate Drone").
- **Ghost:** Text `#697067`, no background, no border. Hover applies background `#EAE8E0` and text `#292D28`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Default State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#DFDED5`, text `#292D28`, height 36px, padding horizontal `space-sm`.
- **Focus State:** Border 1px solid `#46583B` with an outer non-diffused ring `2px solid rgba(70, 88, 59, 0.15)`.
- **Placeholder:** Text color `#92968D`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Indicators:** Subtle backgrounds with high-contrast text. 
  - Standard/Neutral: Background `#EAE8E0`, text `#697067`.
  - Nominal/Running: Background `rgba(82, 122, 89, 0.12)`, text `#527A59`.
  - Attention: Background `rgba(193, 138, 66, 0.12)`, text `#C18A42`.
  - Failure: Background `rgba(185, 83, 77, 0.12)`, text `#B9534D`.
- Borders on chips must remain 1px solid, matching their respective text color at 25% opacity.

### Cards & Modules
- Structured on a pure `#FFFFFF` background with a mandatory 1px border (`#DFDED5`).
- Internal module headers must have a bottom border of 1px solid `#DFDED5` and an internal padding of `space-md`.

### Data Grids & Tables
- Table header row sits on `#EAE8E0`, text styled via `label-sm` in `#697067`, all uppercase.
- Table body rows utilize alternating pure `#FFFFFF` and subtle row states on hover (`#F4F2EC`).
- Grid division rows use hairline dividers of 1px solid `#DFDED5`.
- Numeric data cells consistently implement tabular numerals and right-align against header labels.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Unchecked: 1px border `#89977A`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked: Background `#46583B`, border `#46583B`, checkmark glyph in `#FFFFFF`.
- Radio active center point: 4px solid circle `#46583B`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Telemetry Gauge:** Clean concentric SVG circular arcs using `#EAE8E0` as the baseline track and `#46583B` or `#B8794B` as the dynamic fill. No skeuomorphic glossy dials.
- **Valve/Zone Switcher:** Segmented control track in `#EAE8E0` with individual active segments styled in `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border of `#DFDED5`.
