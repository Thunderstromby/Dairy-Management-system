### 2. Project Scope

The Software Development Company agrees to design and develop the SmartFarm Dairy Management System according to the approved Scope of Work & Quotation Document, Version 1.0.

The project includes three main interfaces:

1. Public SmartFarm Landing Page.
2. Super Admin Panel.
3. Private Farm Management Application for Farm Admins and Farm Supervisors.

The farm management application will include the approved modules for:

- Animal Management
- Animal Health Management
- Milk Production and Management
- Inventory Management
- Feeding Management
- Worker Management
- Attendance Management
- Payroll Management
- Financial Management
- Equipment Management
- Dashboards and Reporting
- Notifications and Automation
- Farm Settings and Data Management

The system will support responsive usage on desktop, laptop, tablet, and smartphone browsers.

Only functionality documented in the approved project scope is included in this agreement.

Features identified as excluded, optional, or future enhancements are not part of the current development commitment.

### 3. Project Deliverables

The Software Development Company will provide:

1. UI/UX designs for the approved interfaces.
2. Responsive public landing page.
3. Super Admin Panel.
4. Private Farm Management Application.
5. Frontend and backend development.
6. PostgreSQL database implementation.
7. Role-based access and farm workspace data isolation.
8. Approved notifications and background automation.
9. Included reports and data export functionality.
10. Functional, responsive, integration, and permission testing.
11. Production deployment to the client-approved hosting environment.
12. Initial Admin account configuration.
13. Source code handover.
14. Basic deployment instructions.

### 4. Project Timeline

The preliminary estimated project duration is **22–31 weeks (approximately 5–7 months)**.

The expected project phases are:

| Phase | Estimated Duration |
|---|---|
| Requirements finalization | 1–2 weeks |
| UI/UX design | 2–3 weeks |
| Platform foundation and authentication | 2–3 weeks |
| Landing page and Super Admin | 2–3 weeks |
| Animal and milk management | 3–4 weeks |
| Health, inventory, and feeding | 3–4 weeks |
| HR, attendance, payroll, and equipment | 3–4 weeks |
| Finance, dashboards, and automation | 3–4 weeks |
| Testing and refinement | 2–3 weeks |
| Deployment and handover | 1 week |

The final schedule will be confirmed after requirements finalization, technical planning, resource allocation, and confirmation of pending business rules.

Delays in client feedback, approvals, content, or required access may affect the delivery schedule.

### 6. Revision Limits

The following revisions are included:

- UI/UX Design: 2 revision rounds.
- Landing Page Design: 2 revision rounds.
- Dashboard Design: 2 revision rounds.
- Development: Bug fixes against the approved scope.
- Final Testing: Corrections against agreed acceptance criteria.

A revision round means one consolidated set of feedback from the client.

Revisions must remain within the approved project scope.

Additional revisions, major design changes after approval, new functionality, or additional modules may require separate charges.

### 7. Client Responsibilities

The Client agrees to:

1. Provide approved project requirements.
2. Provide company branding, logo, and other required assets.
3. Supply website content and product descriptions.
4. Provide accurate farm operational data and sample records.
5. Confirm business rules and calculation formulas.
6. Define and approve user roles and permissions.
7. Provide hosting and domain access.
8. Provide email service configuration and required credentials.
9. Confirm cloud backup arrangements.
10. Review and approve UI/UX designs.
11. Review and test completed modules.
12. Provide consolidated feedback within mutually agreed review periods.
13. Assign an authorized person for project approvals.
14. Provide required legal and privacy-related content.

Delays in fulfilling these responsibilities may affect project milestones and delivery dates.

### 8. Change Requests

Any requirement that is not included in the approved project scope will be treated as a change request.

Examples include:

- New pages or modules.
- New business workflows.
- Major changes to approved functionality.
- Additional third-party integrations.
- Mobile applications.
- Advanced analytics or reporting.
- 3D visualization.
- Other excluded or optional features.

The Software Development Company will review the requested change and provide its estimated cost, timeline impact, and implementation details.

A change request will only be implemented after approval by both parties.

Changes will not automatically become part of the original project cost or timeline.

### 9. Hosting and Domain Responsibility

The Software Development Company will prepare and deploy the application to the hosting environment approved by the Client.

The Client will be responsible for providing the required hosting and domain access.

The following costs are not included in the project price unless separately agreed:

- Hosting subscriptions.
- Domain registration and renewal.
- Cloud infrastructure.
- DNS services.
- Cloud backup storage.

Domain ownership and hosting account arrangements will be handled by the Client.

### 10. Third-Party Services

The project may require external services for email delivery, hosting, cloud storage, or other infrastructure.

Unless explicitly included in the final commercial agreement:

- Third-party subscription charges will be paid by the Client.
- The Client will provide required service accounts and access.
- External service configuration will depend on the selected provider.
- Third-party service availability and service policies are outside the Software Development Company's direct control.

No paid external API dependency is assumed in the current approved scope.

### 11. Approval Process

The project will follow a milestone-based approval process.

1. The Software Development Company will present completed designs or development milestones for review.
2. The Client will review the submitted work and provide consolidated feedback.
3. Design deliverables will include the agreed two revision rounds.
4. Development corrections will be handled against the approved scope and acceptance criteria.
5. Once the Client approves a milestone, the project will proceed to the next stage.

The Client will assign an authorized representative to provide project feedback and approvals.

### 12. Testing and Acceptance

The Software Development Company will perform testing against the approved requirements.

Testing includes:

- Functional testing.
- UI testing.
- Responsive testing.
- Authentication testing.
- Permission testing.
- Data validation testing.
- Approved calculation testing.
- Integration testing.
- Notification testing.
- Regression testing.
- Modern browser testing.

Before final handover, the following conditions must be met:

- Approved modules have been implemented.
- Agreed roles and permissions have been tested.
- Farm workspace data isolation has been tested.
- Approved business calculations have passed testing.
- Included notifications and automation have been tested.
- Responsive testing has been completed.
- Agreed critical and major defects have been resolved.
- Production deployment has been completed.
- Source code and agreed handover documentation have been provided.

New requirements raised during acceptance that are outside the approved scope will follow the change request process.

### 13. Final Delivery and Handover

After completion of development, testing, and production deployment, the Software Development Company will provide:

- Deployed SmartFarm application.
- Source code.
- Initial Admin account setup.
- Basic deployment instructions.
- Agreed handover documentation.

The Client will review the completed project against the approved scope and acceptance criteria.

Final delivery will be completed after the agreed acceptance review and the final payment milestone have been fulfilled.

### 14. Maintenance After Delivery

Post-launch maintenance is not included in the current project price unless separately agreed.

Any ongoing maintenance, technical support, hosting management, feature enhancements, or future development will be discussed under a separate maintenance agreement or quotation.

Development bug fixes against the approved scope are included as specified in the revision and testing provisions of this agreement.

### 15. Cancellation

If either party wishes to cancel the project, the parties will communicate the cancellation in writing.

Both parties will review:

- Work completed up to the cancellation date.
- Deliverables already provided.
- Payments already made.
- Outstanding approved payments.
- Any agreed work or expenses associated with the project.

The parties will discuss and agree on the settlement of outstanding matters before closing the project.

No additional cancellation charges or refund conditions are established by this agreement unless separately agreed in writing.

### 16. Intellectual Property and Source Code

The Software Development Company will provide the project source code as part of the agreed final handover.

The ownership and usage arrangements for the completed project, source code, designs, and any pre-existing third-party components will be confirmed and agreed upon by both parties in writing before final handover.

Third-party libraries, frameworks, and services remain subject to their respective licenses and terms.

### 17. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential business information, project information, credentials, operational records, and other non-public information shared during the project.

Neither party should share the other party's confidential information with unauthorized individuals without prior agreement.

Access credentials and sensitive project information should be handled responsibly.

### 18. Scope Exclusions

The following items are not included in the current project:

- Public customer self-registration.
- Subscription billing and online checkout.
- Automatic recurring payments.
- IoT and RFID hardware integration.
- Offline data entry and synchronization.
- Individual worker login accounts.
- Advanced accounting and reconciliation.
- Advanced payroll overtime calculations.
- Advanced breeding management.
- Advanced nutritional recommendation engines.
- Automatic inventory unit conversion.
- Advanced audit tracking.
- Advanced custom report builder.
- Maintenance cost tracking and reminders.
- 3D farm environments and models.
- Dedicated Android and iOS applications.
- Paid external API dependencies.
- Languages other than English.
- Any functionality not documented in the approved scope.

These items may be considered separately through additional discovery and quotation.

### 19. Pending Requirements

The parties acknowledge that some business rules require confirmation before implementation.

These include:

- Multiple farms per customer and multiple Admin accounts.
- User access across multiple farm workspaces.
- Super Admin support-access permissions.
- Milk production drop calculations and missing-data handling.
- Milk sales exceeding available stock.
- Automatic inventory deduction for feeding.
- Nutritional recommendation rules.
- Final payroll formulas and deduction policies.
- Financial calculation rules.
- Breeding requirements.
- Backup frequency, retention, and recovery policies.
- Hosting and email service selection.
- Account activation and password security rules.

These decisions will be finalized during requirements clarification.

Any decision that changes the approved project scope, cost, or timeline will be handled through the change request process.

### 20. Agreement Confirmation

By signing this agreement, both parties confirm that they have reviewed and understood the project scope, deliverables, estimated timeline, payment milestones, responsibilities, revision limits, exclusions, and handover conditions.

The approved Scope of Work & Quotation Document, Version 1.0, forms the basis of this agreement.

Both parties agree to communicate clearly and cooperate throughout the project.
